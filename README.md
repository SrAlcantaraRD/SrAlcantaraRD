### 🧠 Mindset & Philosophy

Curiosity has always been the driving force behind everything I do.

Curiosity makes me ask why. If the answer isn’t obvious, I observe. If observation isn’t enough, I form hypotheses. Then I experiment, gather evidence and refine my understanding until reality confirms — or disproves — my assumptions. That’s how I learn, make decisions and execute them.

I don’t think like an engineer only when I’m designing software. Whether I’m architecting a distributed system or figuring out why water is leaking through a kitchen wall, my approach is the same. Engineering isn’t just what I do, it’s how I make sense of the world.

What motivates me most is building products that solve real problems with measurable impact. Knowing that something I helped create saves people time, removes friction or genuinely improves their daily lives is what makes this profession meaningful.

I also believe software doesn’t exist in isolation. Great products emerge where engineering meets business, design and people. That’s why I’m constantly exploring disciplines beyond software. Every new perspective helps me make better decisions because reality is one, but understanding it requires looking at it from different angles.
